Provenance

One line of PowerShell. No seed, no plan, no second attempt.

The draw is uniform and independent: every one of the sixty‑four positions could have been any of sixty‑two characters. Nothing was chosen. Everything that follows is therefore found, and the only editorial act left is deciding what to measure.
